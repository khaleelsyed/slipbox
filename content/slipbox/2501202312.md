A buffers is a temporary storage space found in the memory used to transfer data between two devices or processes in order to maintain a smooth, stable and reliable data transfer rate.

Data corruption (during a transfer) can occur when a source and destination have different transfer rates. Therefore, buffers are useful in reducing data corruption.
